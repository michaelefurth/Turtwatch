# TurtWatch — Notification Copy

Voice: warm, silly, never guilt-trippy. Emoji-forward. Turtley speaks in first
person sometimes. Keep titles < 40 chars, bodies < 120 chars.

## Daily reminder (no upload yet, default 7:00 PM)
- "🐢 Turtle check-in!" — "Your daily turtle is waiting. Snap one before bedtime?"
- "Pond's calling 🌿" — "One photo keeps the streak alive. You've got this!"
- "Turtley misses you 🐢💚" — "It's been a slow day in the pond. Got a turtle for me?"

## Streak at risk (late evening, ~2h before midnight)
- "Streak SOS! 🛟" — "Your {n}-day streak naps at midnight. Quick, upload a turtle!"
- "Don't break the shell 🐢" — "{n} days strong! A photo now keeps it going."

## Shield auto-applied (next open / silent push)
- "Shell Shield saved you! 🛡️" — "You missed yesterday, but a shield kept your
  {n}-day streak safe."

## Missed day (gentle, next morning)
- "A sleepy pond day 😴" — "No turtle yesterday — that's okay! Repair it or start fresh today."

## Milestone celebrations
- 7 days: "One whole week! 🐢🎉" — "+25 Turtbux. Turtley is doing a happy shell-wiggle."
- 30 days: "30-day turtle legend! 🏆" — "+100 Turtbux and a shiny new rank await."
- 100 days: "100 turtles!! 🐢💯" — "Pond Sage status incoming. +500 Turtbux!"
- 365 days: "A YEAR of turtles 🥹🐢" — "You magnificent shell-keeper. +2000 Turtbux!"

## Turtbux / shop nudges (low frequency, opt-in)
- "Your Turtbux jar is full 🪙" — "{n} Turtbux ready to spend in the shop."
- "New sticker drop ✨" — "Fresh pond stickers just hatched in the shop."

## Fact of the day (opt-in)
- "Did you know? 🐢" — "Tap for today's turtle fact (and +2 Turtbux)."

## Re-engagement (lapsed 3+ days, max 1 / few days)
- "The pond is quiet 🌙" — "Turtley left the light on. Come back whenever you're ready."
- "Your shells miss you 🐢" — "No pressure — your streak history is safe. Pop in?"

## Copy rules
- Never shame ("you failed", "don't lose"). Frame around the turtle/pond.
- Personalize {n} only when the value is real and ≥ 2.
- Respect quiet hours and per-type opt-outs from Settings.
- Cap: 1 daily reminder + at most 1 risk + 1 optional nudge per day.
